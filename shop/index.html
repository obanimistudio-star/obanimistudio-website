A few useful details

Is this a physical product?

No. These are downloadable files. Nothing is posted to you.

Can I edit or fill in the planners?

Edit the DOCX files in compatible word-processing software. PDFs are designed for printing and writing by hand; they are not interactive forms. Word layouts may vary between apps.

Is personalisation included?

These are ready-made templates. Customisation, personalisation and individual planning services are not included.

What does the licence allow?

One purchaser may edit and print the templates for personal use or internal use in one business or community organisation. Resale, redistribution and public sharing of the files are not permitted.

Can I download on my phone?

You can save files on a compatible phone or tablet. A ZIP download may need to be extracted in your Files app. Editing DOCX files requires a compatible app.

What if there is a problem with my order?

Email obanimistudio@gmail.com with your order reference and a description of the problem. Do not send payment-card details. We can help with missing or faulty files.
