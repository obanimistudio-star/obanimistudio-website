A cleaner recording

A simple setup makes more difference than heavy effects.

1
Choose a quiet room

Close windows and move away from fans, televisions and hard reflective walls.

2
Connect headphones

They prevent the instrumental from leaking back into the microphone.

3
Keep a steady distance

Sing about a hand-span from the microphone and slightly to one side.

4
Use the count-in

Let the four beats establish your tempo before you begin singing.

5
Trim and master

Select the useful section on the waveform, then create your mastered WAV.

Studio workflow: first capture a clean take, then press Enhance & master. The original always remains available. Mastering works best for songs under eight minutes and cannot repair a microphone that was distorted while recording.

Your browser may encode audio as M4A, MP4 or WebM depending on the device. For important recordings, download each take immediately and keep a second copy.
