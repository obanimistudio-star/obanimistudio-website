# ObanimiStudio

Official website of **ObanimiStudio**, an independent creative studio in Bristol, UK, creating thoughtful websites, useful digital products, music and distinctive visual design.

## Live website

**https://obanimistudio.com**

## What we create

- Responsive websites and digital experiences
- Digital products and learning resources
- Visual design, flyers and social-media graphics
- Music and creative work through ObanimiSound

## Brand identity

The black, gold and white **OS logo** is the primary ObanimiStudio identity. It is used across the website, app icons and social metadata.

## Hosting and deployment

The website is hosted with **GitHub Pages** and published from the `main` branch. The custom domain is configured through `CNAME`.

## Project structure

- `index.html` — main website
- `styles.css` and `refinement.css` — responsive visual design
- `script.js` — site interactions
- `manifest.webmanifest` — installable web-app settings
- `service-worker.js` — offline/static caching
- `assets/brand/` — official brand assets
- `assets/icons/` — favicon and app icons
- `robots.txt` and `sitemap.xml` — search-engine discovery
