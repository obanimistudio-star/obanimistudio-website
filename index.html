Before we begin

A few useful answers.

How much will my website cost?

Starter websites begin at £350, professional organisation sites at £750 and premium platforms at £1,200. The final quote depends on your pages, features, content and launch requirements. See the packages and payment terms above.

How long does a project take?

We agree a realistic delivery schedule after discussing your requirements. Timing depends on the project size, the availability of your content and how quickly feedback is provided. Include your preferred launch date in your enquiry so we can confirm what is achievable.

Do I need a domain or hosting already?

No. We can help you choose and set them up. Domain registration and any paid hosting or third-party services are discussed separately and agreed before purchase. Your quote will explain what is included and any recurring costs.

Who owns the website and accounts?

Ownership and handover are agreed in your project scope. For client websites, we aim to register the domain and hosting in your name so you retain control. Any third-party software, fonts or media remain subject to their own licences.

What should I provide?

Your business or organisation name, contact details, the pages or features you need, and any existing logo, text and photographs you have permission to use. If you need help preparing content, we can include that in the quote.

Can I request changes or ongoing support?

Website packages include two reasonable revision rounds within the agreed scope. Additional revisions, ongoing content updates and maintenance are quoted separately, so you can choose the level of support you need.

How do payments work?

The current schedule is 50% to begin, 25% after main design approval and 25% before final launch. We confirm the scope, payment schedule and any additional costs before work starts.
