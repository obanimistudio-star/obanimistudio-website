Privacy

Clear, respectful handling of your information.

Last updated: 13 November 2026. ObanimiStudio is an independent creative technology studio based in Bristol, United Kingdom.

Information you choose to send

The project enquiry form prepares an email in your own email application. The website does not store the form contents. If you email or call us, we use the details you provide to respond, discuss your project and keep appropriate business records.

Website operation

The site may use essential browser storage for features such as your selected appearance theme and service-worker caching. These features help the website remember your preference and load reliably; they are not used for advertising.

Third-party services and links

Links to services such as YouTube, email providers and client project websites take you to third-party platforms with their own privacy practices. Domain hosting and technical service providers may process limited technical data, such as IP addresses and server logs, to deliver and protect the website.

How long information is kept

Enquiry and project records are kept only for as long as reasonably needed to respond, deliver agreed work, meet legal or accounting responsibilities and resolve disputes.

Your choices

You may ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete information when no legal or contractual reason requires us to retain it.

Contact

For privacy questions, email obanimistudio@gmail.com.
