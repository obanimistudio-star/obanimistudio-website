Accessibility

A digital experience designed to include more people.

Last updated: 14 November 2026. ObanimiStudio aims to make this website clear, usable and accessible to as many people as reasonably possible.

Our approach

We use readable text, meaningful headings, keyboard-friendly controls, visible focus styles, alternative text where images convey information, responsive layouts and reduced-motion support. Visitors can also choose dark, bright or cool appearance modes.

Standards

We work toward the principles of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at Level AA. This statement describes our intentions and current approach; it is not a claim of formal certification.

Known limitations

Some older third-party content, embedded media or linked services may not provide the same level of accessibility as the main website. We review the site as it develops and aim to correct barriers that we can control.

Tell us about a problem

If you cannot access information or complete an action, email obanimistudio@gmail.com. Please describe the page, the difficulty you experienced and the device or assistive technology you were using. We will respond and look for a reasonable alternative.
