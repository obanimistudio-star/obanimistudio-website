Privacy Policy — Ṣé o mọ èdè Yorùbá?

Last updated: 28 September 2026

ObanimiStudio provides Ṣé o mọ èdè Yorùbá? (the Yoruba knowledge quiz, also presented as Ọgbọ́n Yorùbá). This policy explains how information is handled when you play the quiz.

Information used by the quiz

You can play without creating an account. The current quiz saves your best score, question rotation and display theme on your device using local storage. These settings help you resume the experience and avoid repeating questions. ObanimiStudio does not receive those locally stored values through the quiz.

You may optionally type a name for a certificate. The name is used on your device to generate the certificate image you choose to download; the quiz does not send that name to ObanimiStudio. A certificate saved to your device is controlled through your device's file settings.

Website delivery and messages

If you open a web-hosted version, the hosting provider may process technical information needed to deliver the site, such as your IP address and browser request details, under its own privacy practices. The quiz itself has no account service, advertising or analytics integration in the version described here.

If you email us for help, we receive the information you choose to send, including your email address and message. We use it to answer your request and do not use it to advertise to you. Email providers process those messages to deliver and store them.

Sharing and retention

ObanimiStudio does not sell your quiz data. The locally stored quiz settings remain on your device until you clear the app or browser storage. A certificate remains in your downloads until you delete it. Support emails are kept only as needed to answer requests and maintain necessary records.

Children

The quiz may be used by children. It does not require a child to register or provide contact details to play. Children should ask a parent or guardian before contacting us by email. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will review the request.

Your choices

You can clear the quiz's saved score, question rotation and theme by clearing the app's or browser's storage in your device settings. You can delete certificates you downloaded using your device's file manager. For questions about an email you sent or to request its deletion, contact us below.

Changes and contact

We will update this page if the app's features or data practices change, including if advertising, analytics or accounts are added. Please check the last updated date above.
